# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good intent while addressing impact, respect differing viewpoints, and focus critique on ideas and work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, and publishing private information without consent. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation proportionately. Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer; response contact details must be configured by repository administrators.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant. This short project policy is not a claim of formal adoption of a particular Covenant version.
